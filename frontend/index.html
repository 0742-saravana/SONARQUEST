National Marine Debris & Anomaly Detection Initiative

AI-Powered Automated Underwater Debris & Anomaly Detection System

Automating Side-Scan Sonar (SSS) acoustic inspection to accurately detect, segment, and geotag hazardous man-made debris, ghost nets, shipwrecks, and subsea pipelines against complex natural seafloors.

System Overview

Four Core System Pillars

Designed to overcome high acoustic speckle noise, varying resolutions, and acoustic shadows in seafloor logs.

1. Object Detection / Segmentation Model

AI/ML architecture (YOLOv8-seg, Faster R-CNN, U-Net) trained to identify and draw precision bounding boxes and masks around man-made hazards including ghost nets, shipwrecks, subsea pipes, and metallic cylinders.

2. Confidence Scoring & Noise Filtering

An algorithmic pre-processing pipeline featuring Lee Despeckling and acoustic highlight-shadow analysis that minimizes false alarms caused by natural sand ripples and rock clusters, generating calibrated 0% to 100% confidence scores.

3. Anomalous Reporting & Geotagging

Translates acoustic slant-range coordinates and AUV ping headers into global geodetic Latitude/Longitude coordinates (WGS84). Generates structured CSV and JSON inspection manifests detailing exact locations and target dimensions.

4. Operational UI Dashboard

Clean, interactive visual interface where users can upload raw sonar logs, inspect detections with bounding boxes on the seafloor map in real-time, and download annotated JPG images and CSV anomaly reports.
